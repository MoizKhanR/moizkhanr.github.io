One extra shift shouldn't cost you your stay

HourGuard counts your work hours against your student visa's limit, live, using the official rules for Canada, Australia and the United Kingdom.

What it does

  • Hours left, always. One number that knows your country's rules: rolling 7 days, rolling fortnights or Monday weeks.
  • Can I take this shift? A clear yes, tight or no before you say yes to your manager.
  • Breaks handled. Scheduled breaks, and Canada's 150- and 180-day rules, worked out for you.
  • Free proof report. A PDF or spreadsheet of every shift, whenever you need it.
  • Private by design. No account. Your records stay on your phone and in your own backup.

Always current

HourGuard checks the official government pages every day and updates its rules without an app update. The current rules file is public: rules.json.

HourGuard is an independent app, not affiliated with any government, and isn't legal or immigration advice.
