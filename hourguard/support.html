Frequently asked questions

How does HourGuard count my hours?

It uses the official rules for your country. In Canada it checks every 7 days in a row, not just Monday to Sunday. In Australia it checks every fortnight starting on a Monday, rolling each week. In the UK it checks each Monday-to-Sunday week. Only paid time counts, not unpaid breaks. Tap the meter on the home screen to see exactly how your hours are counted, with links to the official sources.

Do planned shifts count?

Yes. A shift you've agreed to work counts toward your limit as soon as you add it, so HourGuard can warn you before you work it, not after. Once a planned shift has passed, HourGuard asks you to confirm it or fix the times.

What does "Can I take this shift?" do?

Enter the shift your manager offered and you get a clear yes, tight or no, plus the longest shift that would still fit your limit. Free users get 3 checks a week; HourGuard Pro makes them unlimited.

What about school breaks?

On scheduled breaks from your school's academic calendar, many students can work without the usual limit. Add your breaks in Settings → Breaks, using your school's official dates. Until you add them, every week counts as term time. That's the safe choice: HourGuard can only warn you early, never late. In Canada, a break must be at least 7 days in a row to count.

I already worked before installing HourGuard. What do I do?

During setup, HourGuard asks how many hours you've already worked in the current week or fortnight, so your meter is right from day one. You can also add past shifts any time with Log hours.

How do I get proof of my hours?

Settings → Proof report creates a PDF or spreadsheet of every shift, week by week. It's free, and you can add your name and school to it.

I got a new phone or reinstalled the app. Where are my records?

On iPhone, HourGuard backs up to your own iCloud and offers to restore it when you set the app up again. On Android, Google's automatic backup restores it if backup is on. You can also save a backup file any time (Settings → Save a backup file) and restore it with Restore from a backup file.

What do I get with HourGuard Pro?

Unlimited shift checks, limit alerts while your shift timer runs, and repeating shifts. Your meter, logging, history and proof reports stay free forever. Pro is a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or year ends to avoid being charged. Moved to a new phone? Open HourGuard → Settings → Restore purchase.

The rules changed. Will HourGuard know?

HourGuard checks the official government pages every day. When a rule changes, the app updates itself, without an app update, and shows a notice on the home screen. The date the rules were last checked is shown at the bottom of the home screen.

Is HourGuard legal advice?

No. HourGuard is an independent app that counts hours using published rules. It isn't affiliated with any government. Your own visa or permit conditions always come first. If something doesn't look right, talk to your school's international student office or a licensed immigration adviser.
