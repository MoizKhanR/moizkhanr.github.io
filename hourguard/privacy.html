Privacy Policy

HourGuard - Student Visa Hours

HomePrivacySupport

The short version

  • There's no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your shifts, jobs, study dates and settings are stored on your phone and in your own iCloud or Google backup. We never receive them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The only thing the app downloads is a public rules file, and it sends nothing about you to get it.
  • We don't sell your data. We don't have any to sell.

Who we are

HourGuard - Student Visa Hours ("HourGuard", "the app") is made by Abdul Moiz Khan ("we", "us"). Contact: snsd_forlife@hotmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter in HourGuard is saved on your device:

  • Your study country, course type, course start date and scheduled breaks (used to work out your work-hour limit).
  • Your jobs, shifts, planned shifts and the shift timer.
  • If you choose to add them, your name and school, which are only printed on the proof report you create.
  • Your settings, such as reminders and whether you've finished setup.

Because these records relate to your student visa, we designed HourGuard so that nobody but you holds them. We can't see, recover or hand them over, because we never had a copy.

Your backup

So you don't lose your records if you delete the app or change phones, HourGuard keeps a copy in your own account:

  • iPhone: a compressed copy is saved to your iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. It belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If iCloud is off, nothing is backed up.
  • Android: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android's automatic app backup may copy the app's data to your Google account, covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

We can't access either backup.

Information that leaves your phone

The rules file

Visa rules change, so HourGuard checks for an updated rules file on this website (hosted by GitHub Pages) at most every few hours. The request contains nothing about you or your hours. Like any website, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when the file is downloaded. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Files you share

Proof reports (PDF or CSV) and backup files are created on your phone. They only go where you send them, for example to your email or your school's international office.

Links you open

The Rules page links to official government websites. If you tap one, it opens in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

HourGuard Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see your card details, name or email, only whether Pro is active.

Notifications

Shift reminders, the weekly recap and limit alerts are scheduled by your phone itself. No push server is involved and nothing is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your location.
  • We don't show ads or use analytics, crash-tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information, and we never report anything to any government or school.

Legal bases (GDPR)

We don't receive personal data from the app, so in practice there's nothing for us to process. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the technical request for the rules file is handled by our hosting provider (GitHub) for our legitimate interest in keeping the app's rules current. Your backups are processed by Apple or Google as part of the service you use with them.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Your records are always yours to manage in the app:

  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase all data deletes your records from your phone and from your iCloud backup.
  • Get a copy: Settings → Save a backup file, or create a proof report.
  • Android backup: remove it in your phone's Google backup settings.

For anything else, email snsd_forlife@hotmail.com and we'll respond within one month.

Children

HourGuard is made for international students and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not legal advice

HourGuard is an independent app. It isn't affiliated with IRCC, the Australian Department of Home Affairs, the UK Home Office or any government. It counts hours using published rules and doesn't give legal or immigration advice. Your own visa conditions always come first.

Changes to this policy

If we change how HourGuard handles information, we'll update this page and the date below. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
