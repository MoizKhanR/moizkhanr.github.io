Frequently asked questions

How do I use it?

Start a trip and snap the sale tags as you shop. When you're done, snap the receipt. Price Guard matches each tag to its receipt line and shows anything that rang up higher, with what you may get back.

My receipt is long. Can I take it in parts?

Yes. Take it in two or three photos with a few lines of overlap. Price Guard puts them together and checks that the lines add up to the subtotal. If a part is missing, it tells you which part and roughly how much is missing.

It read a price wrong.

Tap the price to fix it, or retake the photo flat and in good light. Every price is either read clearly or confirmed by you, so a misread can't turn into a claim without you seeing it.

Will the store really give it to me free?

It depends on where you shop. Laws (Quebec, Connecticut, Massachusetts, Michigan, Wisconsin) are enforced by the authorities. Codes and store policies (the rest of Canada, Australia, New Zealand) are voluntary but widely honoured. The Rules page in the app shows your rule, what it doesn't cover, and who to contact if a store says no.

Where I shop isn't listed.

Pick "Somewhere else". Price Guard still finds what rang up higher, so you can ask for the shelf price. If your area has a scanner rule we don't know about, tell us.

What do I get with Price Guard Pro?

Unlimited receipt checks (the free plan has 3 a month), the AI double-check of every catch, store history, and no ads. It's a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or monthly.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid being charged. New phone? Price Guard → Settings → Restore purchase.

I got a new phone. Where are my trips?

On iPhone, Price Guard backs up your trips to your own iCloud and brings them back when you install it again (signed in to the same Apple Account). Photos aren't backed up.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They're read on your phone. The only exception is Pro's optional AI double-check, which sends one tag photo and one receipt line for items that look overcharged. See the Privacy Policy.
