Checkouts get prices wrong more often than you think

A sale that didn't load, a tag that wasn't changed, a coupon that didn't apply. In many places, an item that scans higher than its shelf price can be free. Almost nobody checks. Price Guard checks for you.

How it works

  • Snap the sale tags as you shop. Price Guard reads the price, even from a crooked, blurry photo.
  • Snap the receipt when you're done. In one photo or a few: it puts long receipts back together and tells you if a part is missing.
  • See what rang up higher, line by line, with what you may get back under your local rule.
  • Show the cashier the tag, the receipt line and the rule, on one screen.

Knows the rules where you shop

Canada's Scanner Price Accuracy Code and Quebec's Price Accuracy Policy; Connecticut, Massachusetts, Michigan and Wisconsin; Australia's Scanning Code; New Zealand's supermarket policies. Everywhere else, it tells you to ask for the shelf price. The rules update without an app update: rules.json.

Private by design

No account. Your photos are read on your phone, and your trips stay on your phone and in your own iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.
