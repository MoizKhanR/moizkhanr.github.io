How to use these

  • Save the three photos to your phone's Photos (press and hold, then Save), or show them on another screen and photograph them.
  • In Price Guard: Start a shopping trip, pick a store, then Choose a tag from your photos and pick the shelf tag.
  • Tap I've paid. Check my receipt and add the two receipt photos, one after the other.
  • The Dove Beauty Bar was $4.00 on the shelf and rang up at $5.99: Price Guard shows it, with what you may get back.
