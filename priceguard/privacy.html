The short version

  • There's no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos of shelf tags and receipts are read on your phone. Your trips are stored on your phone and in your own iCloud backup. We never receive them.
  • If you use Pro's AI double-check, one tag photo and one receipt line are sent to be read a second time, and are not kept.
  • The free plan shows a small ad from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier if you allow it. Pro has no ads.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Price Guard ("the app") is made by Abdul Moiz Khan ("we", "us"). Contact: snsd_forlife@hotmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your photos of shelf tags and receipts. The text in them is read on your phone by Apple's or Google's built-in text recognition. The photos are not uploaded anywhere, except as described under "AI double-check" below.
  • Your trips: the stores, prices, receipt lines, your answers to the quick checks, and what you got back.
  • Your settings, such as where you shop and whether you've finished setup.

Your backup

So you don't lose your trips if you delete the app or change phones, Price Guard keeps a compressed copy in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage (photos are not included). It belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. On Android, Android's automatic app backup may copy the app's data to your Google account if backup is on, covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We can't access either backup.

Information that leaves your phone

AI double-check (Pro, optional)

When it's on, and only for items that look like an overcharge, Price Guard sends three things so they can be read a second time: a small copy of that item's shelf tag photo, a strip of the receipt photo showing only that one line, and the text we read on that line. With them goes a random number made by the app when it was installed, used only to apply a daily limit; it isn't linked to you.

They go to our server (run on Cloudflare), which passes them to Google's Gemini API, gets the answer and sends it back to your phone. We don't store the photos or the text. Google may keep the request for a limited time to prevent abuse, under the Gemini API terms; it isn't used to train Google's models. You can turn the AI double-check off in Settings.

Ads (free plan)

The free plan shows a small banner from Google AdMob on the home and history screens. To show and measure ads, Google's ads software may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, and information about how ads are shown and tapped. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow it when asked ("Allow apps to request to track"); you can change this in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), you're asked for consent first. Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads never see your photos, receipts or trips. Pro has no ads.

The rules file

Store rules change, so Price Guard checks for an updated rules file on this website (hosted by GitHub Pages) every few hours. The request contains nothing about you. Like any website, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Purchases

Price Guard Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see your card details, name or email, only whether Pro is active.

Things you choose to send

If you share your savings or email us from the app, it only goes where you send it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your precise location.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-tracking services.
  • We don't sell your information, and we never share your photos, receipts or trips with stores, advertisers or anyone else.

Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies: the AI double-check is processed to provide the feature you turned on (contract); personalised ads are shown only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; and the rules file request is handled by our hosting provider for our legitimate interest in keeping the app's rules current.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, Australia's Privacy Act or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase all data deletes your trips and photos from your phone and from your iCloud backup.
  • Ads: change tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or reset your advertising identifier.
  • AI double-check: turn it off in Settings.

For anything else, email snsd_forlife@hotmail.com and we'll respond within one month.

Children

Price Guard is for shoppers and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Price Guard handles information, we'll update this page and the date below. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
