Frequently asked questions

How do I log food?

Type what you ate the way you'd text a friend, like "2 eggs, toast and a latte", or tap the microphone and say it. Ladle splits it into separate foods and works out the calories.

What's a "custom meal"?

Ladle knows more than 30,000 foods and logs those instantly, for free. When it meets something it doesn't know, it works it out with AI. That's a custom meal. The free version includes a few custom meals a day, and Ladle Pro makes them unlimited. Anything worked out once is remembered, so it logs instantly the next time.

How do I fix a wrong entry?

Tap the food in your log to change its name or calories, or swipe left to edit, split or remove it.

How is my calorie goal calculated?

From the age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal you entered during setup, using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. With Ladle Pro, the smart goal also adjusts every week based on your weigh-ins.

What does Ladle Pro include?

Unlimited custom meals, a smart goal that adjusts to your results, a fasting timer (including Ramadan sehri and iftar), weekly and monthly trends, more meal ideas, and no ads.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ladle. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. If you cancel during the free week, you won't be charged.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Open Ladle, go to Settings and tap Restore purchase. Your food log lives on your old phone, because Ladle doesn't upload it.

Does Ladle work offline?

Yes. Everyday foods log instantly without internet. Custom meals wait for a connection and fill themselves in automatically once you're back online.
