Privacy Policy

Ladle - Calorie Counter

PrivacySupport

The short version

  • There's no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your food log, goals, weight and profile are stored only on your phone.
  • When a food isn't in Ladle's built-in catalog, the words you typed for that meal are sent to our server to work out the calories with AI. Nothing else is sent with them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. You can say no to tracking, and Ladle Pro removes ads completely.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Ladle - Calorie Counter ("Ladle", "the app") is made by Abdul Moiz Khan ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: snsd_forlife@hotmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Ladle is saved on your device and is not uploaded to us:

  • Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal (used to calculate your calorie and macro targets).
  • Your food log, history, weigh-ins, fasting schedule and food preferences.
  • Foods the app has learned from earlier AI answers, so they log instantly next time.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We can't recover it, and we never had a copy.

Information that leaves your phone

Meals worked out by AI

Most foods are matched instantly on your phone. When Ladle doesn't know a food, it sends the text of that meal (for example "detox juice 600ml"), any answer you gave to a follow-up question, and your cooking-oil setting to our server. Our server asks Google's Gemini AI for the calories and macros and sends the answer back.

To keep the service fair and prevent abuse, each request also carries a random ID that the app creates when it's first installed. This ID isn't linked to your name, your Apple or Google account or your advertising ID. We only use it to count how many AI requests a phone makes each day, and those counts are deleted every day.

We don't store the meal text on our server. Google processes it under the Gemini API Terms. Please don't type personal details (like names or health conditions) into the food box.

Voice input

If you tap the microphone, your phone's built-in speech recognition turns what you say into text: Apple's on iPhone, or your device's speech service on Android. Depending on your device, that audio may be processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Ladle receives only the resulting text, and never stores or sends your audio.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as your IP address, device type and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. On iPhone, Ladle asks before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads aren't personalised. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ladle Pro subscribers don't see ads.

Subscriptions

Ladle Pro is purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see your card details, name or email, only whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your location.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-tracking services.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it except as described above or when required by law.

Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process the meal text sent for AI estimates to provide the feature you asked for (performance of a contract). We use the daily request counter for our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and keeping the service running. Personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings. Our service providers (Google and Cloudflare) may process data outside your country under safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because your log lives only on your phone, you can view, edit and delete it in the app at any time. For anything else, email snsd_forlife@hotmail.com and we'll respond within one month.

Children

Ladle isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Health disclaimer

Calorie and nutrition values in Ladle are estimates for general information. They aren't medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Ladle handles information, we'll update this page and the date below. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
