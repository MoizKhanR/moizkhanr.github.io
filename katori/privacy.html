Privacy Policy

Katori. Last updated November 24, 2026.

The short version

Your readings, meals and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up and no server. We never receive your health information, and we cannot see, sell or share it, because we never have a copy.

The free plan shows ads from Google. The ads never receive your health information. The Pregnancy Pass and the monthly plan remove them.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter (glucose readings, meals, medicine doses, notes, your due date, your targets, and the optional name and clinic you add to a report) is saved in the app's private storage on your phone.

  • The app does not upload it to iCloud, to any other cloud service or to us.
  • If you have your phone's own backup turned on (such as iCloud Backup), that backup includes the app's data like any other app's. It belongs to your phone account and is covered by your phone maker's privacy policy, such as Apple's. We can't access it.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data on your phone. You can also delete everything in the app: Settings, Erase all data.

Information that leaves your phone

Reports and backup files you share

The doctor report (PDF or spreadsheet) and backup files are created on your phone. They go only where you choose to send them, for example to your clinic, your email or your Files app. Once you send a file, the place you sent it to looks after it.

Purchases

The Pregnancy Pass and the monthly plan are bought through your phone's app store, which handles the payment. We never see your card details, name or email address, only whether a purchase is active.

Reminders

Testing reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No push server is involved and nothing is sent to us.

Ads on the free plan

The free plan shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of two screens. To show an ad, Google's software in the app sends Google the information any ad request needs, such as your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), the type of phone, app and ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and its privacy policy.

  • The app never gives Google, or any advertiser, your readings, meals, medicine, targets, due date, notes or anything else from your log.
  • We ask Google for non-personalised ads only. The app never asks to track you and never uses your phone's advertising ID.
  • We ask Google for ads suitable for general audiences and turn on Google's restricted data processing setting.
  • We don't choose individual ads. An ad is not advice from us and not medical advice, even when it is about a health product.
  • Buying the Pregnancy Pass or the monthly plan removes ads completely.

Links you open

The app links to the published sources of its numbers (for example USDA FoodData Central, the NHS, NICE, Diabetes Canada and the American Diabetes Association). If you tap one, it opens in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or Apple Health access.
  • No artificial intelligence services: nothing you type is sent to one.
  • We never sell, rent or share information, and we never pass anything from your log to insurers, employers or advertisers.

Your rights

Because your information stays on your device and we never receive it, there is nothing held by us to access, correct or delete. For anything Google holds from showing ads, you can use the controls at My Ad Center. You remain in full control: you can view, change, export (Settings, Save a backup file) or erase (Settings, Erase all data) your information at any time. This applies under laws such as PIPEDA in Canada, the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact
