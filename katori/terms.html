1. What the app is

Katori ("the app") is a personal logbook. It records the blood glucose readings and meals you type in, adds up published carbohydrate values for the foods you choose, compares each reading with the target you entered, and creates reports of what you logged.

2. What the app is not

  • It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition.
  • It does not give medical, nutritional or dietary advice, and it never recommends what to eat, which medicine to take or what dose to use.
  • It does not interpret your readings. "Inside" or "outside" only means whether a number you typed is below a target you typed.
  • It is not a replacement for your doctor, midwife, dietitian or diabetes clinic. Your targets, testing times and every decision about your care come from them. If the app and your care team ever disagree, follow your care team.

3. The numbers in the app

Carbohydrate values are published reference values for a standard portion, taken from the sources named in the app and listed on its Sources page (Settings, Sources, and the "Sources for every number" link on every screen with numbers). Real foods, recipes and portions differ, so these values are estimates. The suggested starting targets are general figures published in clinical guidelines, shown with their source; they are not targets for you. Foods you add yourself carry the number you typed and are not checked.

We work to keep the values accurate but make no promise that every value is correct, complete or current.
