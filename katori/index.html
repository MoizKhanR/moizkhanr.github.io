The pregnancy sugar log that knows what a roti is.

Katori is a simple logbook for gestational diabetes. Log your readings in seconds, log meals by the roti, the katori or the slice, and hand your doctor a clean report. In English and Urdu.

What it does

  • Fasting and after-meal readings, compared with the targets your care team gave you
  • Meals built from South Asian and everyday foods, each carb number with its published source
  • Reminders when it's time to test after a meal
  • Insulin or tablets: note each dose you take. The app never suggests a dose.
  • A reminder for your sugar test after the birth
  • A week view like the paper logbook clinics hand out
  • A doctor report as PDF or spreadsheet
  • No account and no sign up. Your log never leaves your phone.
  • Free, with a small Google ad that never sees your log. The Pregnancy Pass removes it.

A logbook, not a doctor. The app never gives medical advice. Always follow your doctor or diabetes clinic.

Questions

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Save a backup file now and then (Settings, Save a backup file), and restore it on a new phone with Restore from a backup file.

Where do the carb numbers come from?

USDA FoodData Central and the NHS (Cambridge University Hospitals) leaflet on diabetes in pregnancy and Pakistani diet. The app's Sources page (in Settings, and one tap away under every screen with numbers) lists every source with a link.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Manage or cancel in your app store's subscription settings (on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions). On a new phone, open the app, Settings, Restore purchase.
